$cat philosophy.txt

IPNOTEC.VIP is a private, members-only future networking platform. You join with a phone number and a date of birth, choose a future identity (persona + Future City + username), pay a clean 0-and-1 price, and enter a network that has no spam, no ads and no public profiles. Inside: PINGG, the intranet communicator, and one IPNOTEC-hosted members meet every month in every Future City.

privacy first
No email, no social login, no contacts, no passwords. Login is phone + OTP.
server is the law
Rules that protect money, identity or privacy are enforced in the API and database, never only in the browser.
0 and 1 pricing
Rs 10,001 and Rs 1,001, GST included. The bank statement shows exactly that. No fees, no rounding.

The prototype IPNOTEC_VIP_COMPLETE_v4.html shows look and flow only. Everything in it is simulated. If code and this spec disagree, the spec wins.

payment policy

  • No refund, non-transferable. Mandatory tick-box before payment, repeated in Terms.

$cat support.policy

The one and only support address: ping@ipnotec.vip
  • Every active member gets a system-generated alias equal to their ID in lowercase, e.g. ipb.blr.0001@ipnotec.vip. It is not collected from the user and exists only to identify them to support.
  • Only an active member's username@ipnotec.vip can send email support. Accept only if From matches an active alias AND the mail is DKIM-signed by ipnotec.vip AND passes SPF and DMARC (strict alignment).
  • Everything else is rejected at the gateway with a polite bounce: “Support is available to active IPNOTEC.VIP members from their IPNOTEC ID address.”
  • Members send from the in-app “Email Support” button through the authenticated relay. Replies arrive in the in-app Support thread. Tickets are tagged with member ID, type, city, persona.
  • Suspended or lapsed members get only the in-app “Billing help” form. On the 21st-birthday persona change the alias updates and the old one retires.
